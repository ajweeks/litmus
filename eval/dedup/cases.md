# Duplicate-detection eval: cases

Each case seeds the known claims, runs the extractor on the passage, and checks which known claim (if any) the new claim is marked a duplicate of. A duplicate's verdict is copied without a new check, so **a wrong merge is the costly error**; a missed merge only costs one extra check.

30 cases: 12 should merge (3 of them borderline, where either answer passes), 18 should not.
